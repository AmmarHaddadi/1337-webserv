The assembly line at the Ford Motor Company plant in 1913 was a loud, fast-moving machine.Before this new system, workers built one car at a time, moving around a stationary chassis. It took 12 hours to finish a single Model T, making automobiles a luxury only the rich could buy.Henry Ford changed everything by introducing a moving conveyor belt to pull the car frames down the line. Instead of doing many tasks, each worker stood in one spot and performed just one specific job over and over.Production time for a single car dropped from 12 hours to just 93 minutes. This massive shift lowered the cost of the vehicle, making cars affordable for the working class and changing global manufacturing forever.
